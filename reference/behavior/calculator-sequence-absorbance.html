Concentration check by A₂₈₀

This sequence carries 1 Trp and 1 Tyr, giving ε₂₈₀ = 6,990 M⁻¹cm⁻¹. A 1 mg/mL solution reads A₂₈₀ = 5.363 in a 1 cm path. Divide a measured A₂₈₀ by that figure to get mg/mL — a fast, non-destructive way to confirm a stock actually went into solution.

Values are computed from the primary sequence assuming free (unmodified) N- and C-termini, all cysteines reduced, and standard L-amino acids. Acetylation, amidation, cyclisation, non-standard residues and counter-ions all shift the real figures — treat these as a starting point, not as a certificate of analysis.
