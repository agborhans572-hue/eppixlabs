TechniqueCharge tubes T2 onward with 0.9 mL of diluent first. Transfer 0.1 mL from each tube into the next, mixing thoroughly and changing tips between every step. Every tube except the last is left holding 0.9 mL once it has been drawn from — add an extra 0.1 mL of diluent per tube if you need the full working volume to survive the series.

Worked example: 2 mg/mL down to 0.25 mg/mL, 2 mL final

  1. V₁ = (0.25 mg/mL × 2 mL) ÷ 2 mg/mL = 0.25 mL
  2. Transfer 250 µL stock + 1,750 µL diluent
  3. Serial, factor 4: 2 → 0.5 → 0.125 → 0.031 mg/mL
